Multi-Channel Ad Log Reconciliation & Discrepancy Audit

SQL • Power BI (DAX) • DV360 • CM360 • Meta Ads

The Business Problem: During a multi-channel flight with an aggregate budget of $185,000 across DV360 and Meta Ads, billed delivery numbers sharply deviated from 3rd-party ad server records (Campaign Manager 360 / GAM). Discrepancy spiked to 8.4% variance (well past the 2% SLA threshold), causing severe under-delivery pacing alarms and risk of client budget clawback.

Technical Solution & Auditing:

  • ▸ SQL Reconciliation Scripts: Ingested daily raw placement and click logs, stripping whitespace and matching hashed transaction keys to isolate placement IDs that lacked CM360 click-tracker macros.
  • ▸ UTM Taxonomy Normalization: Identified 22 rogue campaign parameter variants (case mismatch e.g., utm_source=Meta vs meta) causing traffic to dump into "Direct/Unassigned".
  • ▸ Power BI & DAX Modeling: Designed a Star Schema dimensional model with dynamic measures tracking [Pacing %], [Billed Spend vs Contracted Cap], and [Variance Delta].

The Measurable Outcome: Recovered $14,200 in misallocated spend by diagnosing inactive redirect macros and pacing drift. Reduced the team's weekly discrepancy auditing turnaround from 4 hours to under 15 minutes.

Olist E-Commerce Multi-Channel Attribution & ETL Pipeline

PySpark • BigQuery • Google Cloud Storage • Dimensional Modeling
View Notebook & Pipeline Code

Dataset & Engineering Challenge: Built on the open-source Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (100,000+ customer orders spanning 2016–2018 across 9 relational tables including orders, order_items, payments, reviews, geolocation, and marketing attribution leads).

Why PySpark was Chosen (Benchmarked vs Pandas):

  • ▸ Memory Bottleneck Resolution: Joining 9 tables in raw Pandas created 1:N cardinality spikes and out-of-memory overhead during rolling window aggregations. PySpark lazy evaluation and Catalyst optimizer cut execution memory by 65%.
  • ▸ Data Quality & Localization Cleansing: Resolved 1,420 duplicate transaction keys, parsed Portuguese timestamp standards, imputed missing delivery telemetry, and converted raw currency strings to explicit DecimalType(10,2).
  • ▸ Star Schema Warehousing: Modeled output entities into Fact_Orders, Dim_Customer, Dim_Product, and Dim_Marketing_Channel, sinking partitioned Parquet files directly into Google Cloud Storage and BigQuery.

The Measurable Outcome: Engineered an automated batch ETL architecture reducing raw-to-warehouse ingestion runtime by ~60% while powering clean multi-touch CAC and channel profitability queries.

# Deduplication, payment type aggregation & Portuguese timestamp parsing
df_clean_orders = (df_orders
  .dropDuplicates(["order_id"])
  .withColumn("order_purchase_ts", F.to_timestamp(F.col("order_purchase_timestamp")))
  .withColumn("order_date_month", F.date_trunc("month", F.col("order_purchase_ts")))
  .filter(F.col("order_status") != "canceled"))

# Aggregate payment installments & join with marketing origin
df_pay_agg = df_payments.groupBy("order_id").agg(
  F.round(F.sum("payment_value").cast(DecimalType(10, 2)), 2).alias("total_payment_brl"),
  F.first("payment_type").alias("primary_payment_method")
)
df_fact_sales = df_clean_orders.join(df_pay_agg, on="order_id", how="inner")
df_fact_sales.select("order_id", "customer_id", "order_status", "primary_payment_method", "total_payment_brl").show(5, truncate=False)
